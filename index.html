Toxic Language and Social Endorsement on YouTube News

2026
BSc dissertation, University of Warwick

Using 1.6 million comments on 4,912 videos from four major US news channels, I find that toxic comments receive about 19% fewer likes than similar comments in the same discussion: toxic content can hold attention while lowering endorsement.
